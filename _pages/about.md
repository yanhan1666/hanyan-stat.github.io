I am a Research Officer in the Department of Statistics at the London School of Economics (LSE), working with [Professor Qiwei Yao](https://stats.lse.ac.uk/q.yao/). My research interests include various aspects of statistics and machine learning, particularly in time series analysis and deep learning. Prior to joining LSE, I obtained his PhD in statistics at Peking University in 2025, under the supervision of Professor [Song Xi Chen](http://songxichen.com).
